# Satzzxzxx Labs — editorial and attribution policy

Applies to blog articles, technical research, project case studies, and app/website evaluations.

## Status gates
IDEA → RESEARCH → EVIDENCE COLLECTED → DRAFT (noindex) → EDITORIAL REVIEW → APPROVED → LIVE VERIFIED → PERIODIC REVIEW.
Only approved genuinely useful pages enter sitemap/feed. Existing draft placeholders stay publicly labeled as drafts and marked noindex.

## Engineering posts
Required: true problem statement, versions/environment/date, original implementation or experiment, reproducible method, evidence (commits, tests, screenshots when relevant), limitations, source citations, publisher identity, conflicts and meaningful update notes.

## App and website reviews
Required: actual product/version/date/platform used; tested functions; transparent limits; supported pricing/privacy assertions; original visual or observation evidence; fair comparison criteria; relevant upstream sources; disclosed competition, ownership and paid affiliations; correction route.

Never label individuals or products fraudulent, malicious, illegal or 'scammers' based solely on rumor or unverified claims. Discuss documented allegations carefully and distinguish subjective concerns from independently confirmed findings. Do not publish exploit instructions, sensitive security weaknesses or private data without coordinated disclosure.

## Project records
Original repo, fork, upstream PR, maintainer review, deployment, and user traction are separate claims. Add direct links to genuine evidence. A product website is not a verified release, an upstream fork is not proof of accepted contribution, and a GitHub organization is not proof of legal incorporation.

## Corrections
Capture exact claim, URL, source, date and correction. Update the affected page and explain material changes where appropriate. No purchased reviews, fake endorsements, AI mass article farms, invented author credentials, trust badges or fabricated financial/security outcomes.

RESEARCH & ENGINEERING

Evidence before claims.

A research notebook for software architecture, reliability, deployment, security engineering and developer tooling, distinct from marketing articles or product announcements.

Research themes

Infrastructure: hosting trade-offs, static sites, CI/CD and recovery. Software quality: accessibility, test coverage, privacy boundaries and reproducible defects. Developer tools: automation, open-source ecosystems and measured performance. Financial topics may appear when they are directly related to software implementation rather than personal financial advice.

How research becomes publishable

Each investigation needs a question, observed source or test environment, method, outcome, uncertainty, limitations, date and links to replicable artifacts when safe. Hypotheses stay labeled as such. Benchmarks will not be invented, cherry-picked or exposed with personal data.
